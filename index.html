About Us

HKS Dance has been bringing the joy of dance to our community for over 20 years. We offer classes for all ages and skill levels — from tiny tots taking their first steps to adults rediscovering the dance floor. Our experienced instructors create a welcoming, supportive environment where everyone can grow.

Our Classes

We teach a wide range of styles including Ballet, Jazz, Contemporary, Hip Hop, and Latin. Classes run throughout the week with flexible scheduling for kids, teens, and adults.
