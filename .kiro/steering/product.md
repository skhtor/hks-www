# Product: Dance School Management Platform

A web-based platform for managing dance school operations end-to-end.

## Core Capabilities

- User authentication with role-based access (Admin, Teacher, Customer, Public)
- Customer and household management with dancer profiles
- Class scheduling, timetable, and enrolment management
- Flexible fee calculation engine with tiered pricing and discounts
- Stripe payment processing with subscription support
- Xero accounting integration (contacts, invoices, payments)
- Teacher portal for attendance tracking
- Admin portal for full operational management
- Waitlist management with automatic promotion
- Reporting and analytics (enrolment, revenue, churn, attendance)
- Merchandise management

## User Roles

- **Admin**: Full system access, configuration, reports
- **Teacher**: View assigned classes, mark attendance
- **Customer**: Manage dancers, enrol in classes, view invoices/payments
- **Public**: View timetable and pricing only
